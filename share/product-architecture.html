AI recommends. A human decides.

That is enforced in the gateway and workflow, not left as a guideline. No model call can shortlist, select, offer, hire, or close a role on its own.

What each domain owns

Short version. Specs and branches live in the repo docs.

  • Identity & access

    Branded login, activation, JWT sessions, admin RBAC. Not enterprise SSO for v1.

  • AI platform governance

    Single Vertex egress, versioned prompts, run logging before invoke, advisory-only enforcement.

  • Design system

    Tokens, authenticated shell, dense tables, page templates used by every feature.

  • Hiring & postings

    JD workspace with human approval, finite and evergreen openings, closure rules.

  • Candidate intake

    PDF upload, scan, parse/OCR, dedup, versioned resumes on Applications.

  • Matching & ranking

    Embeddings, vector retrieval, LLM ranking with provenance (resume, JD, prompt, model).

  • Interview pipeline

    Shortlist reasons, scheduling, versioned notes, AI-drafted scorecards needing approval.

  • Decision & offers

    Priority picks, offer lifecycle, onboarding checklist, optional external employee ID.

  • Insight & reporting

    Workload and pipeline views, aging, AI agreement metrics, redacted exports, audit search.

  • Communications

    Internal notify, resurfacing suggestions, priority lane. Candidate email stays gated.
