What’s broken today

Speed and compliance pull in opposite directions. Tools that add AI usually skip the audit trail. Tools that audit everything feel like 2014.

  • Scattered tools JDs in one app, resumes in another, scorecards in a spreadsheet. Nobody has the full picture when a hire is challenged.
  • Ungoverned AI People paste resumes into ChatGPT. There’s no prompt version, no run log, and no way to replay why someone ranked high.
  • Lost candidates A strong runner-up disappears when the role closes. Next quarter someone starts from scratch.
  • Login politics v1 shouldn’t stall on enterprise SSO. HireSphere ships its own branded login; optional employee IDs stay a field, not a dependency.

What HireSphere covers

Not a feature dump: the hiring loop, end to end. Each piece is shippable on its own, but they share one permission model and one AI gateway.

  • Login & access

    Branded username/password, activation, JWT sessions, and admin RBAC enforced on the API, not just hidden buttons in the UI.

  • AI governance

    One gateway to Vertex. Versioned prompts. Every run written to the database before the model is called. AI can draft and score; it cannot shortlist, offer, or hire.

  • Postings & JDs

    AI helps draft job descriptions. A human approves. Finite and evergreen openings, with clear rules for when a role closes.

  • Candidate intake

    PDF upload, malware scan, parse/OCR, dedup, and versioned resumes tied to applications.

  • Matching & ranking

    Embeddings retrieve candidates; an LLM ranks them with score, fit, gaps, and suggested questions, all tied back to the resume and JD versions used.

  • Interviews & scorecards

    Shortlist with a required reason, schedule rounds, keep versioned notes, consolidate AI-drafted scorecards that still need approval.

  • Offers & onboarding

    Priority picks (up to five), offer tracking, onboarding checklist, optional external employee ID when someone joins.

  • Reporting & resurfacing

    Workload and pipeline views, aging, AI agreement metrics, audit search. Strong near-misses stay visible for the next role.

Why this is hard to bolt on later

Anyone can wrap a chat box around an ATS. The durable pieces are the ones you have to design in from day one.

AI never gets the final say

There is no auto-reject or auto-offer path. The gateway and workflow refuse it. That’s a product constraint, not a policy slide.

Every score has a paper trail

Rankings pin the resume version, JD version, prompt, and model. If inputs change, you can see why the number moved.

We own the login

Custom HireSphere sign-in and local RBAC for v1. Faster to brand, easier to ship, no waiting on corporate IdP politics.

How it’s built

GCP end to end. Boring choices on purpose, so ops and security reviews don’t turn into science projects.

FrontendReact 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind v4, served from GCS + Cloud CDN
API & jobsNode.js Fastify on Cloud Run; Pub/Sub for async work
DataCloud SQL PostgreSQL 15, Drizzle ORM, private IP
AIVertex AI Gemini + Vector Search; prompts in a registry
AuthJWT + bcrypt on a custom /login (not enterprise SSO for v1)
DeliveryTerraform, Cloud Build, managed secrets
