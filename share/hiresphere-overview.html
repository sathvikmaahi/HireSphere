Product architecture

Ten domains. One coherent system.

Each domain is independently shippable, but designed as one architecture — so investors see a platform, not a feature checklist.

01 · Identity & access

Custom login you own

Branded username/password experience, activation gates, JWT sessions, and admin RBAC — not Hubble SSO or a white-label IdP.

02 · AI platform governance

Advisory AI, by construction

Governed gateway, versioned prompts, logged runs, feedback loops. AI cannot shortlist, select, or hire on its own.

03 · Design system

Enterprise UI that scales

Tokens, authenticated shell, dense tables, and page templates so every feature ships consistent and fast.

04 · Hiring & postings

JD to open requisition

AI-assisted JD drafts with human approval, finite and evergreen vacancies, governed closure rules.

05 · Candidate intake

Secure evidence pipeline

PDF upload, malware scan, parse/OCR, dedup, and versioned resumes attached to Applications.

06 · Matching & ranking

Retrieve, then judge

Embeddings + Vector Search retrieval, then LLM ranking with full provenance — score, fitment, gaps, questions.

07 · Interview pipeline

Human decisions with structure

Mandatory shortlist reasons, scheduling, versioned notes, consolidated AI-drafted scorecards with approval.

08 · Decision & offers

Select, offer, close

Priority picks (up to 5), offer lifecycle, onboarding checklist, optional external employee ID capture.

09 · Insight & reporting

Operate and prove

Workload and pipeline dashboards, aging views, AI agreement metrics, audit search, redacted exports.

10 · Communications

Keep talent in play

Internal notifications, resurfacing MatchSuggestions, priority lane for selected-not-offered — candidate email gated.

Guarded stages from apply to hire

Every stage transition is permissioned, reason-aware where required, and auditable.
